TREES

Trees are large, perennial woody plants that typically have a single self-supporting trunk and elevated branches. They serve as the earth's lungs by absorbing carbon dioxide and producing oxygen through photosynthesis. Beyond regulating the global climate, trees provide critical wildlife habitats, stabilize soil to prevent erosion, and yield resources like timber, fruit, and medicine. tree is a tall plant that can live a very long time. It has a single stem or trunk and branches that support leaves. Beneath the ground, a tree has a root system that acts as an anchor and stores the water and nutrients the plant needs to grow.One way we distinguish trees from other plants is their thick and rigid ligneous tissues, which we know as wood.
